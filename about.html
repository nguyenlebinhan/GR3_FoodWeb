Our Story & Passion

Bringing Perfect Sips & Delicious Bites to Your Table

Founded in 2016, Food Shop started with a genuine passion for authentic Vietnamese coffee culture. We believe every great day begins with a perfectly brewed cup and a warm, relaxing atmosphere.

From robust slow-dripped Phin Sữa Đá and modern espresso favorites to refreshing peach fruit teas, creamy freeze beverages, and crispy warm Bánh Mì, every item on our menu is crafted to bring people together with honest flavors and heartfelt hospitality.
